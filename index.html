About Mindful Moments

Mindful Moments was born from a simple belief: everyone deserves a quiet space to reconnect with themselves amid the noise of modern life. Our mission is to make mindfulness and meditation accessible, practical, and deeply restorative for everyone, regardless of their experience level.

Whether you are seeking relief from daily stress, a pathway to better sleep, or a moment of morning clarity, we are here to support your journey. By combining expert-led insights with soothing environments, we empower you to nurture your mental well-being—one breath at a time.

Privacy Policy

At Mindful Moments, we believe in protecting your inner peace and your digital privacy equally. This policy outlines how we handle the minimal information we collect.

We only collect the information you voluntarily provide (such as your name and email address) when you subscribe, submit a live review, or contact us. Your saved preferences (like dark mode or active subscriptions) are stored locally in your browser to respect your privacy.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is strictly used to improve your experience on our platform, manage your subscription access, and respond to your direct inquiries. We do not sell or rent your personal data to any third parties.

3. Security & Cookies

We use robust security measures to protect your data. While we use local browser storage to personalize your session, we intentionally avoid invasive tracking cookies or aggressive analytics.
